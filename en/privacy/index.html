Privacy Policy

Versione italiana

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · change history

This is a translation of the Italian policy published at ghedolab.com/privacy. If the two differ, the Italian version prevails.

In short. WorkTracker has no ads and does not sell data. Clock-ins, leave, trips, expenses and receipt photos stay on your phone: GhedoLab does not receive them and cannot see them. Only a few technical or account details leave the device, always for a specific reason, listed below.

1. Who the data controller is

Nicholas Ghedin, an individual developer (natural person) who publishes apps under the name GhedoLab.
Privacy contact: info@ghedolab.com

This policy covers the WorkTracker app for Android and the ghedolab.com website.

2. How WorkTracker works

Individual use (WorkTracker Lite or WorkTracker). All the data you enter stays in the app's database on your phone. Trips are unlocked with a WorkTracker account, identified by your email: it is used only to manage the trial and the unlock, not to move your data to a server.

Use in an organization. A company can install its own WorkTracker server (self-hosted) and link its employees' phones to it. In that case work data is synchronized with the company's server, and the controller of that data is the company, not GhedoLab. For any question about that data, contact your employer or the server administrator.

3. Data that stays only on your device

Clock-ins, work and travel hours, leave and permits, contract schedule and hour bank, jobs and clients with assigned hours, trips with stops and bookings, expenses, photos of receipts and bookings, imported .ics files, reminders, profile picture, work zones and configured NFC tags.

  • Text recognition on receipts and bookings (OCR) happens on the phone with a local model (Google ML Kit): no image is sent out.
  • The search for the nearest airport uses a list of airports included in the app, without connecting to the internet.
  • PDF and Excel reports and the jobs CSV are created on the phone; you decide whether and with whom to share them.
  • The automatic backup to a phone folder (optional, with its own switch in Backup management) saves a copy of the data in the folder you choose, as often as you set. It stays on the phone, unless that folder is synchronized by another app you installed.
  • A safety copy of the data (JSON file) is updated at every clock-in in the app's private storage, so it can be rebuilt if the database gets damaged. It stays on the phone.
  • The app lock uses the phone's fingerprint, face or PIN through Android: WorkTracker only receives the "unlocked yes/no" result and does not read or store biometric data.

5. Location

WorkTracker does not track your movements and does not keep a location history. Location is used only in four cases:

  • Work zones (optional). If you turn them on, when you open the app the phone reads the location once and compares it with the zones you saved, to suggest "Clock in?". The comparison happens on the phone: the location is not sent to anyone and is not saved in a history. Clocking in always needs a tap from you.
  • Automatic clock-in with zones (optional). If you turn it on and grant the "Allow all the time" location permission, WorkTracker uses the location even when the app is closed or not in use, to clock in by itself when you enter one of your work zones and clock out when you leave it. The check is done by Google Play Services on the phone (geofence): the app is only notified when you enter or leave a zone, it does not read the location continuously. The location is not sent to GhedoLab or saved: only the clock-ins remain, and you can undo them from the notification. You can turn it off at any time from Settings → Work zones.
  • Map to choose a place. If you open the map without a place already set (for example to create a work zone) and you have granted location, the map centres on where you are. The location stays on the phone: the address is looked up only after you tap the map or search for a place, and the app saves the point only if you confirm it.
  • Search for the company server (Android 12 and earlier only). On these versions Android requires the location permission to look for devices on the local Wi-Fi network: WorkTracker uses it only to find your organization's server. From Android 13 "Nearby devices" is used instead, declared as not used for location.

You choose the trip addresses yourself, by typing them or tapping a point on the map.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: receipts, bookings, profile picture, optional screenshots for reports.
  • Location (precise or approximate, your choice from Android 12): only for the four cases described above; in the background only for automatic clock-in, if you turn it on.
  • Calendar: listing the phone's calendars and writing trip and absence events, only if you turn the feature on. WorkTracker changes only the events it created and does not read your other appointments.
  • NFC: clocking in by holding the phone near an NFC tag you configured.
  • Local network and nearby devices: finding your organization's server.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: reminders and the "Did you forget to clock out?" alert, also after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint, face or PIN: only if you turn on the app lock.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Calendar events: while the feature is on; when you turn it off you can choose to remove them all.
  • WorkTracker account: as long as the account exists; deleted within 30 days of your request (see section 7).
  • Reports and requests: for the time needed to handle them, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Sentry diagnostics: for the service's retention period, at most 90 days.
  • An organization's server: the organization decides. Deletions stay recorded for about 90 days to align the other devices, then they are removed automatically.

7. How to delete your WorkTracker account

WorkTracker Lite, which uses no account, has nothing to delete besides the data on the phone. If you created a WorkTracker account (with your email):

  1. Write to info@ghedolab.com with the subject "WorkTracker account deletion request", stating the email you use to sign in to the app.
  2. The request is handled within 30 days.

What is deleted: email, first and last name, trial or unlock status and licence history from the licence service. What is not: the data on your phone, in your Google Drive and in your calendar, which GhedoLab does not hold. To remove it, uninstall the app (or delete it from Settings), delete the "WorkTracker Backups" folder from Drive and turn off the phone calendar choosing to remove the events.

8. Transfers outside the European Union

The licence service and Resend's sending servers are in the EU. Google (reports, Play Store updates, Drive backup, Android backup, Google calendar if you choose it), Sentry and Resend are companies based in the United States: transfers to them are made under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Telegram (a service based outside the EU) receives only the notification of a new request, without personal data.

9. No ads, no sale of data

WorkTracker contains no ads or advertising trackers, and does not sell or hand over data to third parties for commercial purposes.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to processing based on legitimate interest (arts. 15-22 GDPR), by writing to info@ghedolab.com. If you believe your data is processed incorrectly you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of your country.

For data on an organization's server, contact the organization.

11. Minors

WorkTracker is designed for people who work and is not intended for children under 14.

12. The ghedolab.com website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which records visitors' IP addresses in its technical logs for security reasons. The website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts. It only saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser.
